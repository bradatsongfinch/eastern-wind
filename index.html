New to acupuncture? Here is everything you need to know before your first appointment in Indianapolis.

Does acupuncture hurt? +
Acupuncture needles are hair-thin and nothing like medical syringes. Most patients feel either nothing at all or a brief, gentle warmth, tingling, or dull ache as the meridian point activates — quickly followed by deep nervous system relaxation. Many patients fall asleep on the table.
What happens during the 75-minute first visit? +
Tim never rushes a session. Your first visit includes a thorough conversation about your health history, symptoms, and goals, followed by a classical Chinese medicine diagnostic assessment and your first full acupuncture treatment tailored specifically to your body.
How many sessions will I need to see results? +
Every body responds differently, and many patients notice meaningful relief after their very first session. Acute injuries often resolve within 2 to 4 visits, while longstanding chronic pain or fertility support typically benefits from a structured series. Tim will give you an honest, clear plan on day one.
Do you accept HSA/FSA cards or health insurance? +
Yes — Health Savings Account (HSA) and Flexible Spending Account (FSA) cards are gladly accepted. While we do not bill insurance companies directly so we can focus 100% on patient care, we can provide an itemized superbill for you to submit to your insurer for out-of-network reimbursement.
Where is the clinic located and is parking available? +
Eastern Wind Acupuncture is located at 2915 West 33rd St in Indianapolis, IN 46222 — with easy, convenient parking on-site and quick access for patients traveling from Downtown Indianapolis, Carmel, Zionsville, Fishers, and Noblesville.
